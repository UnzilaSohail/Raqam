The problem مسئلہ

A large share of Pakistan's routine record-keeping still runs on hand-filled paper, in places where connectivity is patchy and cloud OCR is priced in dollars.

The data on those forms decides who got vaccinated, who passed an exam, who received flood relief, whose electricity bill is correct. The person filling the form often writes digits in Urdu-Indic numerals (۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹) as readily as Western ones.

Why not just call a cloud OCR API

  • Cost compounds at scale — a per-request bill in dollars gets worse every time the rupee slips against it.
  • Connectivity is the real bottleneck — flood camps, northern districts and many rural union councils don't have the link a cloud API assumes.
  • Western-digit models silently fail on Urdu numerals.
  • The data is sensitive by default — CNIC numbers, health records, beneficiary lists. Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Act is still in draft, so privacy-by-default is the safer choice now, not later.
  • 100% automation is the wrong goal — a silent wrong digit on a marksheet or a vaccination record is worse than a flagged one. The product is the triage, not just the recognition.

The elections question انتخابات کا سوال

Form 45 is the most tempting pillar and the one to touch last. Discrepancies between Form 45 and Form 47 have been a genuine, actively disputed issue in recent elections, including court directives to publish Form 45 images. That makes it a real digitization target — and the pillar where a well-intentioned tool can do the most damage if it is wrong, or is perceived as taking a side.

Position Do not build for elections first, and do not present Raqam as an official or binding tool at any point. If pursued at all, scope it narrowly as an independent civil-society aid for cross-checking already-published Form 45 images against reported totals — never as a system that produces or certifies results — and only after a specific legal and political review, ideally alongside an established election-monitoring body.

Prove the pipeline in education or health first, where a wrong digit costs a re-check rather than a headline.

What the prototype was missing — now built جو کمی تھی

The weekend build was a good learning project. These are the gaps that had to close before it could go in front of a clerk or a health worker.

  • Urdu-numeral recognition — rural form-fillers write ۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹, not just 0–9. One script-agnostic model now recognizes both, trained on MNIST + Perso-Arabic handwriting.
  • Accuracy upgrade path — the 92–96% MLP is not good enough for a marksheet or a vaccination record. A from-scratch CNN plus a hard confidence-triage rule now flags every uncertain digit instead of guessing.
  • Privacy-by-default design — recognition, storage and review all run on-device; only a hash of the source image is kept, never the image. Sync is opt-in per deployment.
  • Offline field app — an installable PWA that runs on a sub-$100 Android and on a laptop, with a local queue and opportunistic sync.
  • Pilot evaluation harness — measures digit error rate before and after review, auto-accept rate, and time saved vs. manual entry.
  • Funding path — an application to Ignite – National Technology Fund, scoped to a single-sector pilot (see Funding).
  • Real form templates & a named pilot partner — still needed from you.

Sustainability پائیداری

An open-core model, so the project isn't permanently dependent on the next grant cycle:

  • Free tier — the offline engine + mobile app, self-hosted, for schools, NGOs and government pilots. This is the trust and adoption layer.
  • Grant-funded — health and disaster-relief pilots, through Ignite and donor/NGO partners.
  • Commercial — a DISCO meter-reading contract cross-subsidizes the free-tier work.

About Raqam رقم کے بارے میں

Raqam (رقم — "figure, number") turns a photo of a handwritten form field into structured data, on ordinary phones and low-cost computers, with no internet connection and no cloud service.

It recognizes both Western digits (0–9) and Urdu-Indic numerals (۰–۹) natively. It is not an official or authoritative record system — it is a review-assist tool. A person confirms every value the model is not confident about before it becomes data.

رقم ایک معاون آلہ ہے، کوئی سرکاری ریکارڈ سسٹم نہیں۔ ماڈل جس ہندسے کے بارے میں غیر یقینی ہو، اسے حتمی شکل دینے سے پہلے ایک فرد اس کی تصدیق کرتا ہے۔

Privacy رازداری

  • No form image or field value leaves this device unless you explicitly sync.
  • Only a SHA-256 fingerprint of each source image is stored — for chain-of-custody, not the image.
  • Sync is manual and per-record — nothing is transmitted until an operator presses Sync; it then sends values and hashes over HTTPS to a server the partner controls.
  • Records → Clear this device wipes the local queue completely.
  • For CNIC / health data, follow the partnering programme's existing consent process.

Open source اوپن سورس

The recognition engine and tooling are open-source and auditable — partners can self-host and inspect every line that touches their data. A managed paid tier (hosted dashboards, DISCO billing integration, support) funds the team.
